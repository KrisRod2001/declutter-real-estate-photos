Best Practices to Prepare Images for Decluttering

  • Use high-resolution, well-lit photos as input.
  • Avoid clutter directly on windows or in complex reflections.
  • Clearly mark or describe which items you want removed if you have preferences.
  • Maintain consistency across photos—apply the same style or level of decluttering for galleries in a listing.

Limitations & Considerations

  • Extreme clutter or deeply shadowed areas may require manual clean-ups.
  • Some items may remove too much detail in textures—always review closely.
  • Decluttering does not replace full staging—if you want furniture or décor, that’s a different service.
  • Over-editing can lead to unrealistic look; balance is key.
